Parser

The shared parser reads the month before the day. These tenants write the day before the month. A booking on 7 April is stored as 4 July. That is parser.

Intake tags a report hint when the browser locale is en-US. That tag is a guess. Reports with that guess stay under parser when they did not come through the booking-v3 form. The cause follows the tenant locale and the form, which is what causeOf already computes.

Hint

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People type the month first. The form then reads the field the tenant's way, day first. The calendar shows the same kind of swap. The bug is the hint, which is what causeOf returns for a form report on that form.

One day

desk-notes.json lists these under oneDayMove. The day moves by one. They were still merged into #4471 and counted inside parser.

  • An invite says Tuesday 12 May. The calendar says Monday 11 May.
  • Every booking in June is one day off, and only June.
  • An invite is timestamped before the booking existed.
  • The same meeting is Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto.
  • Tuesday 6 October shows as Monday.

None of these exchanges the month and the day. The original table footnotes them and still files them under parser.
